Mini-importation—importing smaller quantities of goods for resale—has become a great way for Nigerians to start businesses without breaking the bank. With some capital, entrepreneurs can source products from international manufacturers and have them shipped home for retail. As e-commerce platforms and logistics continue to improve, mini-importation offers a promising path to financial independence for many.
